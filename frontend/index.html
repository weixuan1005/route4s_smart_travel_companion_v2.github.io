Navigation on your wrist

The watch guides each leg: which way to walk, which train to board, the next station, when to change lines and when to get off. It taps your wrist at every one of those moments. When a train is delayed it asks whether to switch route, and when you arrive it asks whether to continue to the next stop. Answer right on the watch. Tap the screen or the crown to flip pages.
